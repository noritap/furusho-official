# Cross-site Photo Reuse Authorization

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-09-18

The site owner explicitly authorizes `furusho-official` to reuse suitable photo assets already published on the owner's other websites when they improve UX and accurately represent the subject/project.

Current approved first-party source:
- `https://noritap.github.io/rhythmspeaker/`

Guardrails:
- Reuse only images that are contextually appropriate.
- Do not infer third-party endorsement from an image.
- Do not fabricate or AI-replace Furusho's appearance.
- Preserve meaningful alt text and intentional crop/focal point.
- Prefer real evidence photography over decorative imagery.
- This authorization does not automatically extend to unrelated third-party remote hosts.

The Visual Asset Registry may classify images served from the approved first-party source as owner-authorized cross-site assets rather than `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
